Vocals Studio

Record or clone a voice, generate a beat, mix & master a song — right in your browser. Local mode is free and private; bring your own AI key for studio-grade voice.

AI-generated audio exports are labelled “ai-generated” in the filename. Only use voices you own or have permission to use.

🎙️ Voice Lab

Local preview works without any key. Record or upload a voice to mix it with a beat.

🥁 Beat Lab

Procedural drums + bass + chords, generated from your mood, genre, BPM, key and seed — same seed, same beat.

🎚️ Mix & Master

WAV export renders offline with compression + normalization. MP4/WebM recording uses your browser's MediaRecorder and is labelled with the actual format it produced.

⚙️ Bring Your Own AI (optional)

For studio-grade voice quality, connect any OpenAI-compatible TTS endpoint with your own key. Keys stay in your browser's localStorage and are sent only to the endpoint you choose. Local mode needs no key.
